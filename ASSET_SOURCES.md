# Recap image source audit

Audit date: September 17, 2026. Scope: the 84 images currently used in Recaps #1–3; unused assets are excluded.

## Required sourcing standard

Use a main image displayed on a Homestuck story page, or a complete frame captured from that page’s Flash animation. Prefer the act being recapped; identify any earlier-act exception. A filename or source link alone does not verify provenance.

An extracted bitmap, background, or character layer inside a SWF is official source material, but does **not** establish that the complete image was ever displayed as that frame. External diagrams linked in comic text do not meet the main-panel requirement. Do not introduce either as a new recap image.

Keep the actual source page in the filename, and record which panel or Flash scene was used. Resize to 650px width with the original proportions, without padding or cropping the artwork. Preserve native animation.

## Findings

- **58 main-panel matches:** compared against image files referenced by the official story pages. All frames were checked, not only the first frame.
- **7 extracted Flash bitmaps:** Recap #3 slides 15, 25, 26, 27, 28, 30, and 32 match artwork embedded in the official Descend SWF. These should be replaced with complete captured frames or main comic panels to satisfy the stricter rule.
- **1 diagram exception:** Recap #3 slide 3 is not the main panel on page 1674. The page links an external Incipisphere diagram in its prose. The current map cannot be approved as a main comic panel or Flash frame.
- **18 existing Flash stills:** their named pages contain Flash content and the identifiable scenes agree with the source material. This audit has not independently reproduced each exact complete frame, so these remain explicitly qualified rather than fully certified.
- **1 act-boundary exception:** Recap #2 slide 18 uses the Midnight Crew animation on Act 3 page 833, rather than an Intermission page. The Intermission opening at 1154 is a different animation; do not simply change the source suffix.

The Rose image on Recap #3 slide 15 matches bitmap 1630 inside the official page-1940 SWF. It was not sourced from a fan-art site. The problem is the use of the standalone artwork layer instead of a verified full animation frame.

No recap images were replaced during this audit. The exceptions below remain visible in the site until suitable replacements are chosen.
