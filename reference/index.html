WHERE THINGS STAND

THE KIDS' SESSION

By the end of Act 4, JACK NOIR has the prototyping ring and has started the RECKONING. Prospit has fallen. Dream Jade is dead, Dream John has awakened, and the kids' own origins have looped back through ectobiology. Jade herself is still on Earth and has not yet entered the Medium.

THE TROLLS' SESSION

Hivebent reveals that the twelve trolls played SGRUB before contacting the kids. They defeat their BLACK KING and successfully create a new universe, which turns out to be the universe containing Earth. Before they can enter it, an apparently invincible demon destroys the portal to their reward. The trolls retreat to a laboratory in the VEIL.

TROLL CULTURE

ALTERNIA

The trolls' home planet. Alternian society is violent, hierarchical, and organized around blood caste. Young trolls live without parents and are raised by animal guardians called LUSII.

HEMOSPECTRUM

Alternia's caste system, determined by blood color. Lower colors begin with rust, bronze, and gold. Higher colors progress through green and blue into purple, violet, and fuchsia. Karkat's bright red blood is a mutation outside the normal spectrum, which is why he hides it.

LUSUS / LUSII

The animal custodians who raise young trolls in place of parents. They range from relatively manageable creatures to enormous monsters. Most of the trolls prototype their dead lusii into their kernelsprites when entering Sgrub.

RECUPERACOON + SOPOR SLIME

Trolls sleep in RECUPERACOONS filled with SOPOR SLIME. The slime suppresses the violent nightmares that otherwise accompany troll sleep. Gamzee also consumes it, which is apparently not what it is for.

TROLLIAN

The trolls' chat client. It can contact people at different points in their personal timelines and supports memo style conversations that can include past and future versions of the same troll. This is every bit as manageable as it sounds.

FLARP

An extreme live action roleplaying game played by several of the trolls before Sgrub. Tavros, Vriska, Aradia, and Terezi's FLARP history becomes a chain of retaliation involving paralysis, ghosts, mind control, blindness, and an exploding magic cue ball.

THE FOUR QUADRANTS

HEARTS: MATESPRITSHIP

Flushed romance. The closest analogue to conventional romantic love.

DIAMONDS: MOIRALLEGIANCE

Pale romance. A close bond in which moirails emotionally balance and pacify one another.

SPADES: KISMESISSITUDE

Caliginous romance. An intense, adversarial bond built around mutual hatred and rivalry.

CLUBS: AUSPISTICISM

Ashen romance. A third troll mediates between two others whose hostility might otherwise become destructive.

GAME + COSMOLOGY

SBURB / SGRUB

Two versions of the same reality altering game. The kids play SBURB. The trolls play SGRUB, which Sollux adapted from code found in Alternian ruins. Starting a session also starts the destruction of the players' home world.

INCIPISPHERE

The self contained game cosmos created for a session. SKAIA sits at the center, with PROSPIT, the player lands in the MEDIUM, the VEIL, and DERSE arranged around it. The FURTHEST RING lies beyond conventional space.

PROSPIT + DERSE

The opposing kingdoms orbiting Skaia. Players have DREAMSELVES on one of their moons. Prospit dreamers can receive visions through Skaia's clouds. Derse dreamers are exposed to voices from the Furthest Ring.

KERNELSPRITE + PROTOTYPING

The kernel released when a player begins entry. Prototyping it with an object or being shapes the player's sprite and also changes the enemies, royalty, and conditions of that session. This is why the kids' Jack Noir inherited such an alarming collection of abilities.

LANDS, GATES + DENIZENS

Each player receives a personal planet and a chain of seven gates connected to their quest. A powerful DENIZEN waits somewhere in that quest structure. The game appears to expect players to develop by completing these individualized challenges.

THE VEIL

The asteroid belt between the player lands and Derse. Its laboratories contain ectobiology equipment, and its meteors become the ammunition for the RECKONING. Both the kids and trolls eventually use Veil labs as major staging points.

THE RECKONING

The endgame meteor assault launched from the Veil toward Skaia. Skaia protects itself by redirecting the meteors through defense portals, often into the players' home world's past and present. This is how the same meteors can both destroy a civilization and create the circumstances that lead to the session.

ECTOBIOLOGY

A process that collects genetic material across spacetime and uses it to create paradox clones. John uses it to create the kids and their guardians. The trolls perform their own version in their session. In both cases, the players are involved in causing their own births.

THE SESSION’S REWARD

The trolls’ completed session created the universe containing Earth. An invincible demon prevents them from passing through the door to claim their reward.

FURTHEST RING

The space beyond the Incipisphere. Strange beings there communicate with Derse dreamers such as Rose. It does not behave like ordinary space, and information can move through it in ways that make an already difficult timeline less helpful.

DOOMED TIMELINES

Alternate branches that fail to sustain the events required by the main causal loop. Dave's failed future in Act 4 and the many alternate Aradias in Hivebent show that doomed versions of players can still act before their timelines collapse.

The Intermission's mobsters mirror the high ranking Dersite agents seen in the game sessions. Different sessions can contain their own versions of these carapacian roles.

JACK NOIR

The kids' session archagent who stole the RING OF ORBS FOURFOLD, killed the Black Queen, and gained the powers of the pre-entry prototypes. At this point, those prototypes are a harlequin doll, Jaspers, a tentacled princess doll, and a crow.

DOC SCRATCH

A mysterious white figure who communicates with Vriska and claims extraordinary knowledge about events. He is extremely polite, extremely manipulative, and very fond of describing himself as an excellent host.

SNOWMAN

The exiled Black Queen from the trolls' session, later associated with the Felt. By the Intermission, killing her would also destroy the universe, which makes ordinary mob violence around her strategically complicated.

LORD ENGLISH

The Felt's unseen boss, introduced during the Intermission. At this point in the reading, his importance is obvious and almost everything else about him is not.
