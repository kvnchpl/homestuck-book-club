HOMESTUCK RECAP #3

Act 4: Flight of the Paradox Clones

Act 4: Flight of the Paradox Clones

The kids’ game takes place in the INCIPISPHERE. At its center is SKAIA, surrounded by golden PROSPIT, the players’ planets in the MEDIUM, an asteroid belt called the VEIL, and purple DERSE. Beyond everything lies the FURTHEST RING.

Each player’s house has seven gates leading through their quest. John and Jade have dreamselves on Prospit’s moon; Rose and Dave have theirs on Derse’s.

John’s FIRST GATE drops him onto LOWAS, the LAND OF WIND AND SHADE. Its salamanders use the PYXIS postal system, which PM helps John operate through her command station.

John orders a fresh hat for Dad and tries to recover his Sburb server disc from Dad’s crashed car. When monsters overwhelm him, Jade’s GRANDPA appears, shoots them, and leaves without an explanation.

Rose’s house has arrived on LOLAR, the LAND OF LIGHT AND RAIN. Mom promptly wanders off, leaving Rose to fight monsters and consult JASPERSPRITE.

Jaspersprite wants her to follow the game’s quest and discover its meaning. Rose is increasingly interested in finding out who made these rules in the first place.

Jade retrieves Dave’s Sburb discs from the lotus time capsule and connects as his SERVER PLAYER. Working through her dreambot while asleep, she prototypes the impaled crow, creating CROWSPRITE.

The sprite immediately steals Dave’s entry item—a red egg—and nests on the radio tower. While Dave’s meteor approaches, Jade upgrades his alchemy equipment. Entry is going about as smoothly as everything else.

Dave climbs the tower to retrieve the egg. Crowsprite attacks, sending both Dave and the egg over the edge.

BRO slices the incoming meteor in half with his katana, and his rocket board catches Dave. The egg cracks, bringing Dave’s apartment into LOHAC, the LAND OF HEAT AND CLOCKWORK.

The trolls become more involved in the kids’ game. gallowsCalibrator (GC) offers John a shortcut to his denizen, TYPHEUS, and helps turn his earlier alchemy experiment into a working rocket pack.

John enthusiastically flies toward his SEVENTH GATE, skipping most of the quest. This is a spectacularly bad idea.

In a DOOMED TIMELINE, Typheus kills John and Jade never enters the Medium. Dave and Rose spend four more months struggling through a session that cannot succeed.

Dave uses his time powers to return to the past and prevent John’s mistake. Rose stays behind and goes to sleep, hoping her dreamself can preserve something of their doomed future.

Future Dave arrives shortly after his younger self enters the Medium. He prototypes himself into Crowsprite, becoming DAVESPRITE, and warns John away from Typheus.

John takes his SECOND GATE instead. The doomed future is left behind; this continuing branch is the ALPHA TIMELINE.

Something of doomed Rose survives: her memories reach the continuing timeline’s Rose, whose dreamself finally wakes on DERSE.

Her walls are covered in the MEOW message Jaspers whispered to her—a genetic code she has been recording without understanding it. She visits Dream Dave, whose unconscious graffiti is, naturally, SWEET BRO AND HELLA JEFF.

John’s Second Gate takes him straight into Rose’s bedroom on LOLAR. She is asleep, so he misses the chance to meet her but finds her unfinished birthday present: a knitted, patched version of the Con Air bunny.

He also impersonates Rose in a chat with grimAuxiliatrix (GA), leaving Rose to untangle the resulting confusion. John takes the bunny, swaps pets with Rose, and flies off before she wakes.

Dave gets hold of Rose’s private writing: her wizard story and a journal containing the MEOW code. He uses his juice-stained Sburb envelopes as bookmarks.

Rose burns her own copy of the code on instructions from the beings in the FURTHEST RING. Derse dreamers hear their voices, just as Prospit dreamers see visions in Skaia’s clouds. Dave’s copy still exists.

The DRACONIAN DIGNITARY—Derse’s counterpart to Diamonds Droog—steals Rose’s journals from Dave’s room. An alternate Dave who tries to stop him gets killed.

Finding his own corpse gives the surviving Dave a very practical lesson in time travel: he has to account for events that already happened, including the versions of himself that did not make it.

Rose increasingly distrusts the game and the fate the trolls predict for them. While Dave advances through his first gate, she destroys hers.

She abandons her walkthrough, sends it into the Furthest Ring, and decides to search for answers on her own terms.

The guardians are also moving through the Medium. Dad receives his fresh hat and meets Grandpa Harley, who has recovered COLONEL SASSACRE’S BOOK. They leave LOWAS through a transportalizer.

Mom and her pony MAPLEHOOF take a similar route from LOLAR. The guardians eventually converge at a laboratory in the Veil, then depart aboard Grandpa’s battleship toward Skaia.

Dream Rose throws LIL CAL out of Dream Dave’s tower. Bro’s wandering rocket board catches the puppet, attracting the attention of the AUTHORITY REGULATOR—the Dersite who confiscated the parcels from Dad’s car.

The Regulator follows Cal through a transportalizer to the same laboratory. He takes the rocket board and goes exploring in the Veil.

John reaches the Veil laboratory after the guardians have left. Its ECTOBIOLOGY equipment can collect genetic material from people it cannot safely retrieve from the past, producing “paradox slime” instead.

The machine targets NANNA, GRANDPA, BRO, and MOM. John collects their samples and starts the cloning process.

The machine makes baby clones of all four guardians. It also combines Nanna’s and Grandpa’s genes to make JOHN and JADE, and Bro’s and Mom’s genes to make DAVE and ROSE.

All eight babies are destined to reach Earth by meteor and grow into the people John already knows. He has just created himself, his friends, and their guardians. Their origins form a closed time loop.

carcinoGeneticist (CG) explains that he performed the same ectobiology in the trolls’ session. He also warns that JACK NOIR will trigger the RECKONING too early and ruin the kids’ game.

John finds time for a Con Air reenactment anyway. The babies acquire the objects that will travel to Earth with them, including Sassacre’s book, Lil Cal, and the bunnies. Crucially, Baby Jade gets the knitted bunny John took from Rose’s room.

Meanwhile, the game’s carapacians have their own stories. On LOWAS, the PARCEL MISTRESS is tracking a green package that Dream Jade asked her to deliver to John.

The Authority Regulator has confiscated it from Dad’s wrecked car, along with John’s Sburb disc. He releases the disc but keeps the package for his superiors. The Mistress follows him to Derse to get it back.

The package ends up with JACK NOIR, Derse’s archagent and the counterpart to Spades Slick. He offers the Parcel Mistress a bargain: bring him the WHITE KING’S and WHITE QUEEN’S crowns, and the package is hers.

He supplies a REGISWORD to help her complete the job. What began as a delivery has become a proposed double regicide.

Jack opens the package himself and finds a weapon powerful enough to kill the BLACK QUEEN, whose humiliating dress code he has had quite enough of.

He takes her RING OF ORBS FOURFOLD and inherits the powers of the kids’ pre-entry prototypings: harlequin, cat, tentacles, and crow. The disgruntled bureaucrat is now the session’s biggest threat.

The WHITE QUEEN willingly abdicates, giving the Parcel Mistress her crown and ring and sending her to the White King for his crown.

The COURTYARD DROLL—the Clubs Deuce counterpart—steals the ring before the Mistress leaves Prospit. Dream Jade catches him, takes it back, and wears it for safekeeping. It gives her no powers. She returns to watch over the still-sleeping Dream John.

At Skaia’s center, the BATTLEFIELD has grown from a chessboard into a whole planet as the kids’ sprites were prototyped. Prospit and Derse are fighting their endless war across it.

When his farm burns, the WARWEARY VILLEIN rallies soldiers from both sides against their rulers. His rebellion marches on the Black King—just as Jack arrives.

Jack kills the BLACK KING, breaks his scepter, and then massacres the assembled rebels. Only the Villein is spared.

Calling himself the SOVEREIGN SLAYER, Jack unleashes the RED MILES across the Battlefield. The rebellion’s hope of ending the war has become a catastrophe.

The WHITE KING surrenders his crown and scepter to the Parcel Mistress. The HEGEMONIC BRUTE—the Hearts Boxcars counterpart—ambushes them, knocking the scepter into a river.

The Droll retrieves it and brings it to Jack. With the white scepter, Jack starts the RECKONING: meteors hurtle from the Veil toward Skaia, long before the kids are ready.

Skaia defends itself by opening portals that redirect the meteors to different points in Earth’s history. Just before the Reckoning, the lab sends all eight babies and their belongings onto separate meteors.

The babies land in the past and grow up to enter—or help others enter—this same game. Other meteors carry the future Exiles to the ruined Earth. The disaster that threatened the kids’ homes is part of the process that created them.

Exploring the Veil, the Authority Regulator finds a meteor carrying an intact version of Jade’s frog ruins. Inside is another ectobiology lab and the lotus time capsule.

Its monitor targets HALLEY, the dog belonging to young Nanna and Grandpa. We see that those two also arrived by meteor, accidentally killed Colonel Sassacre, and were raised by his widow, BETTY CROCKER.

The Draconian Dignitary arrives with Rose’s stolen journals. He puts Dave’s Sburb envelopes into the lotus capsule, where they will wait hundreds of millions of years for Jade to retrieve them.

Then he combines Halley’s genetic material with Rose’s MEOW code. BECQUEREL is born in a burst of green energy. The Regulator witnesses it all: the dog and the discs from Jade’s island both trace back to this laboratory.

The Regulator escapes Bec’s birth and returns to John’s laboratory meteor. John is asleep, the meteor is falling, and its transportalizers no longer work.

He straps John to the rocket board and sends him safely toward LOWAS. With no remaining escape, the Regulator rides the meteor through a portal to Earth.

Jack’s attack reaches PROSPIT. The White Queen escapes aboard a ship, but Jack cuts the chain connecting the planet to its moon.

The moon falls toward the Battlefield with Dream Jade and the still-sleeping Dream John aboard. On LOHAC, Bro also confronts Jack, holding him off in a swordfight.

Dream Jade cannot wake John as their moon falls. At the last moment, she throws him clear and dies in the impact. Her dreambot explodes back on Earth; waking Jade survives.

Dream John finally awakens on the devastated Battlefield. He finds Jade’s body and takes the WHITE QUEEN’S RING from her hand.

The Parcel Mistress kills the Hegemonic Brute and uses his radio to contact Jack. With both white crowns secured, she completes their bargain and finally recovers the green package.

She delivers it to Dream John on the Battlefield. Jade’s seemingly impossible delivery instructions have brought it to exactly the right place and time.

Inside is Jade’s gift: a heavily armed ROBOT BUNNY, built with help from her mysterious penpal. It is the same knitted bunny John gave to Baby Jade, now returned to him after years of upgrades.

When Jack comes for the ring, the bunny protects John and drives him off. The birthday present has completed its loop—and become considerably more dangerous.

Grandpa drops Mom and Dad on the Battlefield, finds Dream Jade’s body, and preserves it according to the family’s unsettling taxidermy tradition.

He returns to Earth before the Reckoning and raises little Jade. The body in his abandoned laboratory, like the FOURTH WALL he took from Jack’s office, is a souvenir of this trip through the Medium.

The EXILES are these same carapacians after reaching Earth: the Warweary Villein becomes the WAYWARD VAGABOND, the Parcel Mistress the PEREGRINE MENDICANT, and the Authority Regulator the AIMLESS RENEGADE. The abdicated White Queen becomes the WINDSWEPT QUESTANT.

At the ruins, AR recognizes WV’s Bec-head pumpkin and remembers the lab. He stops shooting. The group makes peace, builds its little town, and uses the command stations to guide the kids in the past.

When the Questant arrives, WV and AR make her a crown from mailboxes. She declines it and crowns the Mendicant instead, honoring the delivery mission she carried through the fall of both kingdoms.

The PEREGRINE MENDICANT becomes the PROSPITIAN MONARCH. PM has earned another name.

One last secret: WV has been carrying a RING OF ORBS FOURFOLD all along.

The kingdoms have fallen, Jack is loose, and the kids’ origins have looped back into place. But a second royal ring is still out there.

END OF ACT 4.
